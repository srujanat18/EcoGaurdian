🚗 Vehicle Emission Check

This page helps citizens understand emission checking (PUC) and encourages regular checks to reduce air pollution.

✅ Why it matters

  • Reduces smoke and harmful gases
  • Improves vehicle performance
  • Helps keep the city’s air cleaner
  • Encourages responsible driving

🗓 When to do it

Recommended: every 6 months
Do earlier if smoke is visible
