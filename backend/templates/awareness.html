📢 Pollution Awareness

Small daily habits can reduce air pollution. EcoGuardian AI suggests simple actions for citizens and municipal bodies.

🌿 What citizens can do

  • ✅ Use bus/metro or carpool 2–3 days a week
  • ✅ Maintain vehicle & do PUC emission test regularly
  • ✅ Avoid burning trash/leaves
  • ✅ Switch off engine in long signals
  • ✅ Plant native trees (Neem, Peepal, Ashoka)

🏛 What municipal bodies can do

  • 📍 Identify high AQI zones and increase green cover
  • 🚦 Improve traffic flow & reduce congestion
  • 🏭 Monitor industries for smoke & dust control
  • 🧹 Reduce road dust using water sprinklers
  • 📢 Conduct awareness drives & school campaigns
